MOQ Explained: Why Low MOQ Matters

MOQ (minimum order quantity) decides how much cash you tie up. A low MOQ of 10 pcs lets new shops test the market and scale as sales prove out.

Shipping: Local vs Overseas

Local EU stock wins on speed and risk. Overseas freight is cheaper per unit but slow and customs-exposed.

  • Overseas: 2-4 weeks, customs risk, VAT on arrival

How to Choose a Supplier: A Checklist

  • Warehouses in Germany, Poland and Spain for fast delivery
  • Clear communication on WhatsApp and Telegram
