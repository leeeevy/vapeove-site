OEM Private Label Vape: Small Batch Custom Branding from 500 Pcs for EU Retailers

You do not need 5000 pieces to launch a vape brand. HIFANCY OEM starts at 500 pcs per SKU. Custom logo, custom flavors, custom packaging — delivered to your door or our EU warehouses.

Why Small-Batch OEM Changes the Game for EU Retailers

Most factories in Shenzhen will not talk to you unless you order 3000-5000 pieces per SKU. For a retailer testing a new brand in Germany, Spain, or Poland, that is a non-starter. You are not a brand with distribution yet — you are testing whether your logo on a big puff device sells better than a white-label unit.

HIFANCY OEM starts at 500 pcs per SKU. That is small enough to test a market and large enough to justify custom tooling costs. You get the same hardware as the proven HIFANCY SUPER 70K, 60K, 50K and 45K platforms — just with your brand identity on the device, the box, and the flavor labels.

What You Can Customize at 500 Pcs

At the 500-piece entry point, the customization scope is solid but not infinite. Here is exactly what you control:

  • Brand name and logo: Silkscreen or UV print on the device body. Your logo replaces the HIFANCY mark. Vector files required (AI or EPS).
  • Packaging design: Full-color box design with your brand colors, logo, and regulatory text. Die-cut shape stays the same as standard HIFANCY packaging to keep costs down.
  • Flavor selection: Pick 5-10 flavors from the existing HIFANCY library. Need a custom flavor profile? Possible with a slightly higher batch minimum — contact for details.
  • Puff count spec: The hardware determines this. 70K, 60K, 50K, and 45K are the four platforms. Pick the one that fits your price point.
  • Sticker and insert: Compliance stickers, batch codes, and any internal insert cards go with your branding.

What you cannot customize at 500 pcs: the device mold shape (that requires 10,000+ units), the battery spec, or the coil resistance. These stay at factory defaults, which are already market-tested across tens of thousands of units sold in Europe.

The 500-Piece Economics: Branded vs White-Label

A white-label box of HIFANCY 70K from our EU warehouses costs X. An OEM-branded box of the same hardware costs X plus the customization fee, which covers design setup, printing plates, and packaging tooling.

The per-unit premium for OEM at 500 pcs is modest — typically 10-20% above white-label wholesale. But the retail premium you can charge for your own brand easily exceeds 30-50% depending on your market positioning.

Here is the real math that matters: selling 500 branded units at a 5 EUR higher retail price than generic units generates 2500 EUR in extra margin. The OEM customization fee pays for itself inside the first batch.

For exact pricing on your batch size and configuration, contact us — prices shift with order volume and complexity.

The OEM Process: From Design to Delivery

The workflow from idea to branded product follows a clear four-step path:

Step 1 — Choose hardware and flavors. Pick from 70K, 60K, 50K, or 45K puffs. Select 5-10 flavors per SKU. We confirm availability and production slot.

Step 2 — Artwork and approval. You send your logo (vector), box artwork (or we design it for a fee), and any compliance text. Our design team prepares production files. You approve digitally before anything enters production.

Step 3 — Production. 15-25 days manufacturing after approval. We send you real photos of the finished units before shipping. Payment terms are discussed at order confirmation.

Step 4 — Logistics. Two options: ship directly to your address (you handle customs), or route through our EU warehouses for customs-cleared DDP delivery to any European address. The Germany warehouse, Poland, or Spain — your branded stock sits in the same logistics network as our white-label inventory.

Is 500 Pcs Right for Your Brand? A Realistic Checklist

OEM is not for everyone. Before committing to 500 units, answer these questions honestly:

  • Do you already sell 200-300 white-label units per month? If you are not moving stock, a brand will not fix that. OEM amplifies existing demand — it does not create it from zero.
  • Can your sales channels support a brand premium? If your customers only care about price and puff count, stick with white-label HIFANCY and enjoy higher volume at lower margin. If they ask "what brand is this?" — OEM is the next step.
  • Do you have a name, logo, and packaging concept ready? You need artwork or a budget for design. We can recommend designers who understand vape packaging requirements.
  • Can you handle 500 units of one SKU? That means committing to one puff count and 5-10 flavors at a minimum. Diversification across SKUs comes later.

If the answers tilt yes, 500 pcs is the smallest serious batch size in the European vape OEM market. No one else offers this entry point on big puff hardware.
