DDP (Delivered Duty Paid) means the shipment price includes all duties, taxes, and clearance fees. VAPEOVE ships from EU warehouses, which means intra-EU deliveries have no customs at all. For shipments outside the EU, DDP ensures your customer receives the package without surprise fees. Either way, the delivery is smooth and predictable — typically 2-5 days within Europe.
