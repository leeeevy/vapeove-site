How to Pay for Vape Wholesale Orders: SEPA, Wise, Crypto Guide for EU Retailers

You have picked your HIFANCY big puffs, confirmed stock at the warehouse — now how do you actually pay? A plain-language guide for resellers in Germany, Spain and Poland.

July 2026·6 min read·Payment Guide

Payment Options: What Works for Your Order Size

Paying for vape wholesale does not need to be complicated. We accept four main payment methods, and the right one depends on your order size, location, and how fast you want stock to move.

For small orders of HIFANCY 70K or 45K — think 1-5 boxes, 10-50 pcs — the fastest options are SEPA or Wise. Funds clear in 1-2 days and stock ships from our EU warehouses the same day we confirm payment. You have product in hand within 3-4 days of sending money.

For medium orders — 50-200 pcs, cross-border EU delivery — Wise and Revolut offer the best exchange rates and lowest fees. If you are in Germany, Spain, or Poland, SEPA from your local bank works exactly the same as paying a domestic supplier.

For larger orders or repeat buyers who want speed, USDT crypto transfers settle in minutes. No bank hours, no intermediary delays, no questions.

SEPA Bank Transfer — The Standard for EU Resellers

SEPA (Single Euro Payments Area) covers all EU countries plus Norway, Switzerland, and the UK. If you have a EUR bank account, SEPA is the default payment rail.

How it works:

  • We send you an invoice with IBAN and BIC details
  • You initiate a SEPA transfer from your bank, Wise, Revolut, or N26
  • Funds clear in 1-2 business days (often same-day for major banks)
  • We confirm payment and release stock for dispatch

SEPA is free or nearly free for most European banks. There are no correspondent bank fees, no currency conversion loss (since EUR stays EUR), and every payment creates a traceable record — useful for your own bookkeeping.

The only downside: bank holidays and weekends delay processing. If you pay Friday evening, stock ships Monday. Plan accordingly if you are ordering for weekend sales.

Wise and Revolut — Faster, Cheaper for Cross-Border

If you do not use a traditional bank or want to avoid your bank's transfer fees, Wise (formerly TransferWise) and Revolut are the go-to options for European vape resellers.

Why resellers prefer Wise:

  • Mid-market exchange rate — no hidden markup
  • Multi-currency accounts — hold EUR, PLN, GBP in one app
  • Instant or same-day transfers within the SEPA network
  • Low flat fees (typically under 1 EUR per transfer)

Why resellers prefer Revolut:

  • Built-in currency exchange at interbank rates
  • Virtual and physical debit cards for supplier payments
  • Instant notifications when payment is received
  • Free transfers within Revolut ecosystem

Both apps are free to register and take under 15 minutes to set up. If you are buying big puffs wholesale in Germany or shipping orders to Spain, Wise and Revolut eliminate the friction of traditional bank transfers.

USDT Crypto — Instant Settlement, No Bank Delays

For resellers who hold crypto or want settlement speed that banks cannot match, USDT (Tether) is our preferred crypto payment method.

We accept USDT on TRC-20 (Tron network, near-zero fees) and ERC-20 (Ethereum network, slightly higher gas). TRC-20 transfers typically confirm in under 60 seconds.

When crypto makes sense:

  • You already hold USDT from trading or other business
  • Your bank limits or flags frequent transfers to new payees
  • You want same-day order processing regardless of the time
  • You prefer privacy — crypto transactions do not include purpose codes or business descriptions

Crypto is not required. It is an option for resellers who want it. If you prefer SEPA, use SEPA. We match the payment method to what works for your operation.

What to Expect After Payment: Stock Release Timeline

This is the part most guides skip. You send money — then what?

SEPA/Wise/Revolut timeline:

  • Day 1: You initiate transfer
  • Day 1-2: Funds arrive and are confirmed
  • Day 2: Order is packed at nearest EU warehouse (Frankfurt, Warsaw, or Madrid)
  • Day 2-3: Courier picks up, tracking number sent
  • Day 3-5: Delivery to your door

Crypto timeline:

  • Minute 0: You send USDT
  • Minute 1-10: Blockchain confirms, we verify
  • Same day: Order packed and dispatched if before 14:00 CET
  • Next day: Tracking number in your inbox

All orders ship from EU local stock. The product you are paying for is already in the warehouse — no production wait, no container delays. Browse HIFANCY 60K or HIFANCY 50K and confirm your payment method at checkout.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I pay by SEPA bank transfer?

Yes. SEPA transfers are supported for EU-based buyers. Funds typically clear within 1-2 business days. No intermediary bank fees within the SEPA zone.

Is Wise or Revolut accepted?

Yes. We accept Wise and Revolut transfers. These are the fastest and cheapest options for cross-border EUR payments. Use the bank details we provide with your invoice.

Can I pay with USDT or other crypto?

Yes, USDT (TRC-20 and ERC-20) is accepted. Crypto payments settle within minutes and avoid banking delays. Wallet addresses are provided at order confirmation.

Do you offer net terms or credit?

For new customers, payment is upfront. After 3-5 regular orders, we may offer 50% deposit / 50% before shipping terms on a case-by-case basis. Contact us to discuss.
