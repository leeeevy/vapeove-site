How to Display Vapes in a Small Store

You've got limited counter space. Here's what works:

  • Countertop acrylic stand. 5 slots, one for each flavor. Put a price card next to it. Customers point, you hand them the sealed box from below the counter. Fast and clean.
  • Wall-mounted strip behind the register. Use a hanging display strip above or beside your cigarette shelf. Customers see it while waiting to pay. Zero counter space used.
  • Glass showcase. If you have a display case, put 3-4 sample units inside with small price labels. Looks premium and prevents theft.

The key: put them where people wait. Checkout line, register area, anywhere customers stand for more than 10 seconds with nothing to do but look around.

Do I need a tobacco license to sell vapes in my store?

Regulations differ by country. In Germany, for example, vapes are not classified as tobacco products and do not require a tobacco license. However, most EU countries require you to verify customer age (18+). Check your local laws. VAPEOVE ships from within the EU, so no import license is needed.
