Cross-Selling Strategies That Work in Tobacco Shops

Your staff already knows how to sell tobacco. Vapes require a slightly different approach, but it's still simple:

  • Natural upsell at the counter. Customer asks for their usual cigarette brand. Your staff says: "Have you tried these? 70,000 puffs, lasts two weeks. A lot of our customers are switching." No pressure, just information.
  • Display next to cigarette shelving. Put HIFANCY units on a small stand directly beside or above your cigarette display. Smokers see them every time they buy cigarettes. Familiarity drives trial.
  • Bundle suggestion. "Grab your usual cigarettes and take a vape for when you're inside or in the car." Dual users buy both — and that's double the transaction value for you.
  • Mint/menthol as the gateway. Cigarette smokers switching to vapes almost always start with menthol or mint flavors. It feels familiar. Stock these prominently and suggest them to customers buying menthol cigarettes.

Don't worry about vapes killing your cigarette business. It won't happen. The global trend is clear — smoking rates are declining slowly, vaping rates are rising, and a large segment of the population does both. By stocking both, you serve every type of customer who walks through your door.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will selling vapes cannibalize my cigarette sales?

No — it adds revenue. Most vape customers in tobacco shops are either dual users (they buy both cigarettes and vapes) or former smokers who switched. Either way, they're walking into your shop because they trust you for tobacco products. If you don't stock vapes, they buy their vapes elsewhere — and maybe their cigarettes too. Stock both and you keep the customer.

What's the margin on vapes compared to cigarettes?

Cigarette margins are thin — typically 5-8% in most EU countries due to high excise taxes and regulated pricing. Vape margins are significantly higher, often 40-60%. A single HIFANCY vape sale at 20 EUR can net you more profit than 3-4 cartons of cigarettes combined. And it takes the same counter transaction time — about 30 seconds.

Do I need new licenses or permits to sell vapes in my tobacco shop?

In most EU countries, your existing tobacco retail license covers vape sales. Regulations vary — in Germany, vapes are regulated under the Tobacco Products Act (TabakerzG) but do not require a separate license beyond what you already have. Always verify with your local authority. VAPEOVE ships from within the EU, so no import permits are needed.
