Proces OEM — 6 kroków do Twojej marki

From concept to delivery. Typical timeline: 4-8 weeks depending on customization complexity.

STEP 1

Consultation

Share your vision via WhatsApp or Telegram. We discuss puff count, flavors, target market, and budget. Get a preliminary quote within 24 hours.

STEP 2

Design & Sampling

You provide logo files and brand guidelines. We create 3D mockups. Once approved, we produce 5-10 pre-production samples for your review.

STEP 3

Sample Approval

Test the samples: flavor accuracy, vapor quality, device feel. Request adjustments. Most brands approve within 1-2 rounds.

STEP 4

Mass Production

Full production run starts after sample sign-off. We send weekly production updates with photos. Typical cycle: 2-4 weeks.

STEP 5

Quality Inspection

Every batch undergoes AQL 2.5 inspection before shipping. You receive QC photos and a detailed report before dispatch.

STEP 6

Delivery & Reorder

Ship to our EU warehouses or your port of choice. Reorder is faster — we keep your mold, design files, and flavor recipes on file.
